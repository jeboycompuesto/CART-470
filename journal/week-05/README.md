## Current stage of the project

In my previous entry, I documented our campus recordings, my panoramic photographs, and the materials I shared on Fizzy. Those assets remain a foundation for the project. The current challenge is connecting them through a usable interface and a working technical structure.

The team's October 19–20 discussion made the next priorities more specific: a base platform, map integration, and an ambisonics demonstration. André proposed starting the platform with Python and Flask, Hugo expressed interest in the map integration, and William offered to set up MongoDB. These are team contributions and proposed responsibilities that help clarify where my design work can fit.

## What I worked on

During reading week, I was busy with midterms, but I kept up with the team's discussions and have been working on ideas for the UI in Figma. My work during this period was mainly ideation and staying connected with the team's direction. I am now catching up on the project and moving toward more concrete design work.

On October 20, I checked in with the group and said I would review the updates, organize our Figma files, and look for ways to improve the current product. I will take responsibility for the Figma UI. I am also happy to help build the product, including supporting the Python/Flask setup and Google Maps or Open Maps integration wherever the team needs help.

Our FigJam board lays out the relationship between Figma, the web interface, audio, photographs, metadata, storage, and a database. It gives us a shared plan for how the pieces might connect. For my part, the useful question is how that structure becomes understandable to someone using the Soundscape Explorer: choosing a place, opening its visual context, and accessing its sound.

## Reflection on the process

The team discussion highlights the importance of making responsibilities concrete. A plan to improve the interface needs a specific outcome, such as a clearly organized screen or a location-selection flow that another teammate can implement. Otherwise, design and development can progress separately without resolving how they work together.

The proposed technology stack also needs to remain a plan that we test. A diagram helps us discuss connections, but a working example will show whether those connections are practical. The most useful near-term goal is one location with its image and audio, supported by an interface that makes the next action clear.

Balancing midterms with the project made this a quieter period for hands-on progress. Keeping up with the team helped me stay aware of the direction, but I now need to turn the UI ideas into something we can review together. Taking responsibility for the Figma UI gives me a clear starting point. For the development work, I want to coordinate with the teammates already handling those areas and agree on a specific task where I can be useful.

## Next steps

1. Take the lead on the Figma UI, organize the existing material, and turn the initial ideas into wireframes the team can review.
2. Work through a clear flow for selecting a location and accessing its panorama and audio.
3. Review the proposed interface with the teammates working on the platform, map, and ambisonics so the design fits what we can build.
4. Coordinate with André and Hugo on where I can help with Python/Flask and map integration, then take on a specific implementation task.

The team's other suggestions include hosting, database work, and eventually an interface for exploring the EV building's floors and spaces. I am open to helping where needed, while keeping the initial UI and core experience as my immediate priorities.
