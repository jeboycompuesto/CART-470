# Week 10 — New campus recordings and photos before the final

**Jeboy Compuesto · CART 470 · November 25, 2026**  
**Project: Concordia Soundscape Explorer**

[Back to journal](../../README.md)

This week, I took part in another campus recording and photography session with the team on Friday, November 20. We went around campus to record new material and take photos for Concordia Soundscape Explorer. With the final approaching, this gives us new material to bring into the project and an opportunity to connect our interface ideas more closely to the places we are documenting.

The session brought the focus back to the content of the experience. Our project depends on the relationship between a location, its visual context, and its sound. A recording and a photo can each document a place, but the interface needs to make their connection clear. Someone choosing a location should understand what they are opening and how to start exploring it.

For my UI work, the next question is how to make the new material fit into the flow from browsing campus locations to entering a soundscape. The design needs enough information to help someone choose a place, followed by clear playback controls and a way to return to browsing. Using our own campus material gives those decisions a concrete context instead of relying only on example content.

One thing this stage highlights is that collecting assets and integrating them are separate parts of the process. Having new recordings and photos does not by itself mean that every location is ready in the website. The next step is to organize the material, identify which files belong together, and check how each set will connect to a point on the map. We also need to verify the audio format and playback rather than assuming that a new recording is already ready for the spatial-audio pipeline.

My earlier accessibility research remains relevant as we prepare the final experience. Clear location names, understandable controls, and an alternative to relying entirely on visual map markers are still useful priorities. I want to keep those considerations connected to the core experience while being realistic about what we can complete before the final.

The main challenge now is coordinating the remaining work so that the content, interface, and playback come together. My next steps are to help organize the new campus media, review how it should appear in the UI with the team, and work through the location-selection and listening flow. I would prioritize a reliable experience using matched recordings and photos, then use any remaining time to refine navigation and presentation.
